How the radar scores

Implied value = TVL × comps multiple × growth × tenure × token odds. The comps multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of traded protocols in the same category (global median when a category has fewer than four traded peers), clipped to 0.02–3.0. Token odds are the share of protocols in the category that already record a token, shrunk toward the global rate. Growth is the seven-day TVL change clamped to −50…+100%, weighted 0.55. Tenure rises from 0.70 to 1.00 over four years listed. No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row.

Validation, as measured

Read honestly: the comps multiple is a weak predictor — a typical error is a factor of about four. The multiples still separate protocols, and the board ranks on the fitted value, not on a claim of precision.

Coverage

What a token would be worth

A protocol’s launch is the token it may issue one day. The board states what that token would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry.
